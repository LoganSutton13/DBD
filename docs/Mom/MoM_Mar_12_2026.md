### Agenda Items

#### 1. Variable Rate Sprayer / Individual Nozzle Control
**Discussion**:
- Discussed current industry status of individual nozzle rate control on large booms vs. smaller robots.
- Consensus that for a 15-foot robot boom, section-based or boom-wide variable rate is acceptable and still a major improvement over traditional 120–135 ft sprayers.
- Noted that many commercial systems (e.g. Raven, other OEMs) still do not have robust individual nozzle rate implementations available in practice.
- Upgrading existing equipment to more advanced controllers/nozzles is expensive and often requires early-adopter risk.

**Outcome**:
- Team remains focused on achieving reliable variable rate at the robot/boom level rather than per‑nozzle in the near term.
- Recognized that smaller, simpler robots can still deliver meaningful precision gains compared to large, traditional sprayers.

**Discussion**:

- Logan confirmed that temporary coordinate offsets used during debugging have been removed from the production code.

**Outcome**:
- Team will remain opportunistic about test days, watching for still, clear conditions and favorable satellite availability.
